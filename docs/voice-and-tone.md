# Voice and tone

Three contexts, one honest voice: direct language, meaningful actions, no unverified claims.

| Context | Voice | Try | Avoid | Principle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Community (forum, announcements) | Casual, encouraging | "What helped it click for you? Share an explanation or ask a follow-up." | "Only experts should answer." | Invite participation; protect people from personal disclosure |
| Support (consultations, student help) | Empathetic, clear | "Choose a time that works for you. You can review it before continuing." | "We will reply within 24 hours." | Offer a clear next step without a service-level promise |
| Reports (grievance, restricted) | Serious, structured, neutral | "Submission is unavailable while recipients and access rules are confirmed." | "Your report is completely anonymous and secure." | State what is known, what is pending and what is disabled |

## Per surface

| Surface | Notes |
|---|---|
| ACCS landing | Community-driven and encouraging up front; show the privacy boundary on the page ("Community access does not grant report access"); never use grievance content in marketing |
| ACCS portal and admin | Neutral and operational; reports stay explicit; role and permission copy follows the table below |
| Meeting At 10 | Direct, dry, short sentences ("We meet at 10. Then we ship.") |

## Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Honest | No promises of anonymity, security, response time or finality; "restricted access does not mean anonymous" |
| Demo content | Use realistic names and references (CCS-2026-0142); never real student data |
| Errors | Say what went wrong and how to fix it next to the field; keep entered values: "Couldn't validate this subject. Try again; your text is kept." |
| Buttons | Verb and object ("Continue", "Discard draft", "Book a time") |
| Destructive confirmation | Name the object; say whether it can be undone ("This cannot be undone.") |
| Required fields | Words, not an asterisk alone |
| Name | **ACCS**; spoken "Access"; expand on first mention |
| No emoji | In UI and system messages |
| No kickers | No small label above a heading; the heading says it |

## State words

Line forms always travel with a word. Use the same words everywhere.

| Line form | Words |
|---|---|
| Solid (open, active, done) | "Open", "Scheduled", "Submitted", "Resolved", "Done" |
| Dashed (pending, draft) | "Draft", "Awaiting confirmation", "Review pending", "Not started" |
| Hatched (taken, restricted) | "Booked", "Restricted", "Restricted review", "Your own records only" |
| Struck (disabled, closed) | "Unavailable", "Cancelled", "Withdrawn" |

A chosen slot is "Chosen", not "Booked": choosing a time is not a confirmed booking.

## Times and dates

| Item | Format |
|---|---|
| Time | 24-hour, tabular: `09:00`; ranges with a hyphen: `09:00-09:30` |
| Date | `Wed 21 Oct`; in full for assistive tech: "Wednesday 21 October" |
| Time zone | Always shown near a time choice ("Asia/Manila (PHT)") |
| References | `CCS-2026-0142`, in the time (mono) style |

## Notifications and privacy

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Neutral: "There's an update to a record you can access." Route to an authenticated, authorized view. Keep names, case categories, narrative, attachments and sensitive status out of email subject lines, push previews and lock screens | Put an allegation, a student identity or a report description in a notification. Treat a role label as permission enforcement, or restricted access as a guarantee of confidentiality |

| Role | Permission scope (proposed) | Identity and confidentiality | Policy gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | Own report drafts and records | Not confirmed; do not promise anonymity | Confirm recipients, consent and self-access |
| Assigned reviewer | Assigned cases only | Identity visibility and redaction unconfirmed | Confirm assignment, delegation and audit rules |
| Community moderator | Moderate community posts | No report access by default | Confirm public-post and moderation rules |
| Administrator | Manage configuration | No automatic permission to read cases | Explicit authorization required for case access |

Safe titles: browser title "ACCS / My reports"; never put report subjects, case details or identities in browser titles, community search, global search, the command palette or notifications.
