# Voice and tone

Source traits: professional, trustworthy, modern, accessible, structured.

| Surface / context | Voice | Example | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACCS landing | Warm, clear, confident | "Your Access to the College of Computer Studies." | Hype, "revolutionary", exclamation marks |
| ACCS forum | Community driven, encouraging | "Nice question. Here is where similar threads live." | Sarcasm, gamified pressure |
| ACCS student support | Empathetic, clear, specific | "We got your request. Dr. Reyes owns it and will reply by Friday." | Vague "we will get back to you" |
| ACCS grievance | Serious, secure, confidential, calm | "Only the grievance committee can see this. You can withdraw it at any time." | Jokes, emoji, public-social language, urgency tricks |
| ACCS admin | Neutral, terse, operational | "3 requests overdue." | Chatty copy, marketing tone |
| Errors (all) | Say what happened, what to do | "Password is incorrect. Try again or reset it." | "Oops!", blaming the user |
| Meeting At 10 | Direct, dry, confident, short sentences | "We meet at 10. Then we ship." | Corporate filler, "passionate team" |

## Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sentence case | Buttons, headings, labels. Proper nouns excepted |
| Button labels | Verb + object: "Create request", "Delete post" |
| Confirmations | State the consequence and reversibility |
| Name | Write **ACCS**; say "Access". Expand once as "Access for College of Computer Studies" |
| Numbers | Digits, with units ("3.2 h") |
| No emoji | Not in UI, not in system messages |
