# Surface brief: ACCS design system world, first proven on the ACCS landing example

Scope: the visual world of `@meetingat10/design-system` (tokens, every component, the showcase site), first proven on `site/app/examples/landing`, then the admin overview, report detail, sign-in, a Meeting At 10 example and the docs pages.

Visitor mode: Persuade (landing, Meeting At 10), Operate (admin, report detail, sign-in), Read (docs).

Audience and job: CCS students and faculty learn what ACCS is and sign in; staff and students complete tasks in the portal; the team reads the docs. Proof is the interface at work with synthetic, labeled content (DEMO, sample). Constraints: ACCS palette and fonts pinned; no kickers or eyebrows; honest copy; WCAG 2.2 AA target; reduced motion and forced colors supported.

Memorable moment: the now-line crossing today's column of a ruled week sheet, with the reports lane hatched and labeled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: ACCS is a ruled week sheet. Everything arrives as a block placed in time, and the restricted reports lane is drawn into the grid, hatched and labeled, never hidden. Refuses the edu-SaaS hero: headline, two buttons, card stack, three icon columns.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-white sheets on a cool canvas; hairline rules that are real, labeled time grids; solid full-strength blocks (navy scheduled, teal open, violet community) with time codes in tabular JetBrains Mono; highlighter washes for selection; hatching for restricted or taken; door-plate headers (navy plate, white Plus Jakarta Sans). State in line form: solid open, dashed pending, hatched taken, struck disabled, always with a word.

STORY: The visitor sees a college week already living in ACCS, reads community, consultations and reports as three lanes with a real boundary, and signs in or browses the community.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: ACCS door plate, Plus Jakarta Sans 800 headline at display scale, one supporting line, the primary "Sign in" block beside "Browse the community". Right seven columns: this week's ruled grid (Mon to Fri, 08:00 to 17:00) with labeled synthetic blocks, a hatched "Reports: your own records only" lane, and the now-line crossing today.

FORM: Faculty-door consultation timetable, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 94a7f4c0.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the now-line glides across today in real time; choosing a block lifts it and dims its neighbours one step; picking a slot draws a highlighter swipe from the left.

MOTION GRAMMAR: blocks drop into their cells on a snappy spring; indicators slide along rules on a smooth spring; overlays rise from their trigger with an exponential ease-out; color and hover changes take 120ms. Reduced motion: instant state changes, static now-line.

Raised by the declined challengers:
- Emission Rail: status lives in line form (solid, dashed, hatched, struck), always with a word, never hue alone.
- Event Display: choosing a block isolates it; neighbours dim one step.
- Tensegrity Column: docs anatomy pins each label to its part on leader lines.
- Build Instructions: everything snaps to one row module; new blocks arrive bright while settled ones recede.
- Lowbrow Panel: color owns whole blocks at full strength, never sprinkled accents.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
