## Rules specific to Phase 2

- Overlays (Modal, Drawer, Popover, Menu, Tooltip, Toast) use `overlay-shadow` and `card-bg`; Modal and Drawer use the `scrim` treatment as the backdrop.
- Tooltip is the inverse surface (`text-primary` fill, `canvas` text) like Toast; never put interactive content or essential information only in a tooltip.
- Stepper and Pagination expose state in text (`aria-current`, "Step 2 of 4"), never color alone.
- EmptyState is open content (tile, heading, text, one action), not a card.
